## A. Working thesis

## The frame
Walmart+ acquisition runs on two marquee benefits (free grocery delivery from store, no-minimum shipping); retention runs on a long tail members rarely join for but hate to lose. The known gap: no sustainable third marquee benefit. The membership recently flipped cost-center → profit-center, so every growth idea must survive margin math.

## The thesis: habituality
Retention is decided long before renewal — in whether the membership becomes a weekly habit. Define the habit state explicitly (N uses across M consecutive weeks, per segment) and manage **habituated-member share** as the north star: retention measured while you can still act on it.

The membership should behave like an agent relationship — it learns why you joined, routes you to your value, makes that value felt, survives failures, catches drift, and deepens. Leaving should feel like firing the agent that knows your household.

**Six loops:** LEARN (capture the join reason day zero) · ROUTE (one clear path to the anchor benefit) · REWARD (make savings legible) · RECOVER (failure + generous fix retains; failure + friction churns) · SUSTAIN (detect drift, re-engage) · DEEPEN (trust ladder: propose → approve → automate; cross-category graduation).

**Four gates on every intervention:** point-of-use reliability · supply capacity · exploration quota · margin sustainability.

**Three member modes:** Maya (pays money to save time) · Dan (spends time to save money — never hide decisions from Dan) · Ruth (access-dependent; reliability *is* the product).

**Flip condition:** if agent-routed cohorts don't beat holdouts, Walmart+ needs better surfaces, not an agent spine.

## What I need to learn first (top 5 data asks)
1. **Join-reason capture** — does it exist today, and where does it live?
2. **Trial cohort curves** — activation and conversion by entry channel.
3. **Habit-state definitions in use** — how does the team define an engaged member now?
4. **Delivery-failure recovery flows** — what happens after a failed first order, and what does recovery cost/save?
5. **Renewal cliff shape** — churn timing around promo expiry and annual renewal.
